Our story

SHOQAN started in 2018 as a small tailoring atelier on the left bank of Astana. We named it after Shoqan Valikhanov, a scholar and traveller who was known for his sharp, careful style.

Today we sell suits, shirts and coats made from Italian and Turkish fabrics, and every item can be fitted in our atelier for free.

Frequently asked questions

We shorten sleeves and trousers in our atelier within 3 days. Visit the boutique with your SHOQAN purchase for a fitting.

Courier delivery costs 2 000 ₸ and is free from a 50 000 ₸ product subtotal before discounts. Boutique pickup is always free. Kazpost costs 3 000 ₸. Choose a method at checkout to see the total.

You can return an item within 14 days if its tags are still on. Contact the boutique for help with a return.

Open the size guide, compare your body measurements, and choose a size on the product page before adding it to the cart.
